In short: ClassCall+ keeps your classroom information on your own device. There are no accounts, no ClassCall+ servers holding your records, and no sale of student information. You can delete your records at any time inside the app.

1. Introduction

Welcome to ClassCall+.

ClassCall+ is an educational and classroom management application developed by Salted Multimedia. It helps teachers organize classes, manage student information, encourage classroom participation, monitor attendance and keep track of student marks.

At Salted Multimedia, we respect the privacy of our users and the students whose information may be entered into ClassCall+.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled by the application, how it is used, where it is stored and how users can manage or delete their information.

2. Information handled by ClassCall+

ClassCall+ allows users to enter and manage classroom information, which may include:

Student information

  • Student names
  • Class lists
  • Attendance records
  • Student marks and scores
  • Reasons for awarding or deducting marks

Classroom information

  • Class names and details
  • Subjects
  • Lesson information and schedules
  • Classroom participation records

Files and exported information

When users use supported export or sharing features, classroom information may be processed to generate files that users can save or share.

ClassCall+ does not require users to create an account or sign in to use the application.

3. How we use information

Information entered into ClassCall+ is used to provide its classroom management features, including:

  • Creating and managing classes
  • Adding and organizing student names
  • Randomly selecting students for classroom activities
  • Recording student attendance
  • Awarding or deducting student marks
  • Keeping track of student scores and attendance
  • Managing lesson timers and classroom activities
  • Displaying relevant classroom information
  • Generating and exporting classroom records where supported

The information is intended to support the user’s classroom management activities.

4. Data storage

ClassCall+ is designed to store classroom and student information locally on the user’s device. This means:

  • Student names and classroom records are stored on the device where they are entered.
  • ClassCall+ does not use an online account system.
  • The application does not provide online backup or synchronisation of classroom records.
  • Classroom information is not automatically uploaded to a central ClassCall+ server.

Users should take appropriate precautions to protect their phones and computers, because information stored locally may be accessible to anyone who gains unauthorized access to the device.

5. Sharing of information

Salted Multimedia does not sell student names, attendance records, marks or other classroom information to third parties.

Classroom information may be shared when users deliberately choose to export or share their records through supported device features or external applications.

Once information is shared through another application or service, its handling becomes subject to that service’s own privacy policy and practices. Users are encouraged to share student information only with authorized individuals.

6. Advertisements and third-party services

The current version of ClassCall+ does not display advertisements.

Advertising may be introduced in a future version of the application. If advertisements or advertising services are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant advertising providers and any information they may collect or process.

Any third-party services incorporated into future versions of ClassCall+ will be reviewed and disclosed where required.

7. Analytics and diagnostic information

ClassCall+ does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. The app does not send usage statistics or diagnostic information to Salted Multimedia or to any third party.

Google Play may provide Salted Multimedia with aggregated technical reports, such as crash and performance data, from users who have chosen to share that data with Google. These reports do not contain classroom records.

If analytics or crash reporting is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

8. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by ClassCall+ against unauthorized access, misuse, loss or disclosure.

Since classroom information is stored locally, users are also encouraged to:

  • Keep their devices protected with appropriate security measures.
  • Avoid sharing their devices with unauthorized persons.
  • Protect exported files containing student information.
  • Avoid sharing classroom records with individuals who are not authorized to access them.

No electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data retention and deletion

Classroom information remains on the user’s device until it is deleted through the application or otherwise removed from the device.

ClassCall+ lets users delete classes and student records within the application. Users are encouraged to review their classroom records periodically and remove information that is no longer needed.

Exported files or copies previously shared through external applications may remain outside the control of ClassCall+ and may need to be deleted separately.

Because ClassCall+ does not require user accounts and does not provide online synchronisation, there is no central ClassCall+ account database from which classroom records can be retrieved or deleted.

10. Children’s privacy and student information

ClassCall+ is designed as a classroom management tool for teachers and other educational users. It is not directed at children, and children are not expected to use it on their own.

Teachers may enter information relating to students, including children and minors. Teachers and other authorized users are responsible for ensuring that they have the appropriate authority to enter and manage student information and that they handle it responsibly.

Users should enter only the information needed for classroom management. ClassCall+ is not intended to encourage the unnecessary collection of sensitive student information.

11. User rights and responsibilities

Users are encouraged to maintain accurate classroom information and manage their records responsibly.

Depending on applicable data protection laws, individuals may have rights to:

  • Request access to their personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Raise concerns about the handling of their information
  • Exercise other applicable data protection rights

For privacy-related questions or concerns, contact Salted Multimedia using the details in section 14.

12. Third-party applications

ClassCall+ may allow users to share or export information through other applications installed on their devices.

Salted Multimedia does not control the privacy practices of external applications or services. Users should review the privacy policies of those services before sharing personal or classroom information through them.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Salted Multimedia may update this Privacy Policy when new features are introduced, when data-handling practices change or when necessary to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Any changes will be reflected in the updated policy, together with a revised effective date. Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
